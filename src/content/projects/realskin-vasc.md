## The challenge

REAL Skin Vasc is a clinic with two specialties under one roof: aesthetic
medicine and vascular surgery. The site had to feel as calm and premium as the
clinic itself, build trust in the doctors before the first visit, and make
booking an appointment the obvious next step on every screen.

## What I built

I developed the site from James Alonso's design, keeping its editorial look
down to the last detail.

- **Trust first.** The doctors and their credentials come right after the hero,
  followed by the clinic's Google rating, real reviews and a before-and-after
  slider patients can drag.
- **Two areas, one site.** Aesthetic medicine and vascular each get their own
  page with treatments split by facial, body and hair, and a technology page
  where the equipment can be filtered by area.
- **Always one tap from booking.** A floating pill navbar, an "Agenda tu cita"
  button in every section and a WhatsApp shortcut that follows you down the page.
- **Plain HTML, no framework on the client.** Built with Astro and Tailwind,
  with small vanilla scripts for the sliders and the menu, so the page stays
  light even with large photography.
- **Careers page.** A "Trabaja con nosotros" page so the clinic can receive
  résumés from the same site.
