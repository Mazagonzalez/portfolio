## The challenge

RoofPixel sells design and development to founders, so its own site is the
first thing a client judges. It had to feel premium in the first few seconds,
explain a fixed-price offer with no fine print, and turn a visit into a lead
without a sales call.

## What I built

- **Static first, animated where it counts.** I built it with Astro and used
  React islands only for the pieces that move, so the rest of the page ships as
  plain HTML and loads fast on any phone.
- **One site, two languages.** English and Spanish live under `/en` and `/es`,
  and visitors land on the one that matches their browser.
- **A brief instead of a contact form.** Clients pick what they need and their
  budget from chips, so every lead arrives already qualified.
- **Mobile as a first-class layout.** Big type, cards and the pricing block are
  redesigned for small screens, not just stacked.
