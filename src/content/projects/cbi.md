## The challenge

Centro Bíblico Internacional has two campuses, several Sunday services, a youth
night and a lot happening in between: devotionals, sermon series, events, worship, small
groups and missions. Newcomers needed to find a service near them in seconds,
and members needed one place to keep up with everything else.

## What I built

- **A home page that answers "when and where".** Right after the welcome, each
  campus shows its service times with a direct link to directions, followed by
  the church's purpose, its pastors and its values.
- **A page for every ministry.** Worship, CBI College, events, series, missions
  and the daily Bible-in-a-year devotional each have their own page, built from
  the same set of sections so they feel like one site.
- **Campus pages that get people there.** Address, map and a WhatsApp button
  for every campus, plus a floating WhatsApp shortcut on every page.
- **Giving made simple.** A donations page with every way to give, reachable
  from the footer of any page.
- **Static and fast.** Astro renders the content as HTML and only the sliders
  and the menu load JavaScript, as small React islands.
